Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to toggle its answer, double-click any question to collapse all answers, or click an input box to highlight its question.

1. How do I create a student account on the learning portal?

Use your university email address on the Sign Up page and verify your account via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.

2. Can I access course materials offline?

Yes, lecture slides and downloadable assignments can be saved locally for offline reading directly from each module.

3. How are assignments graded and returned?

Instructors grade submissions within 5 business days and publish scores along with rubric feedback inside the Grades tab.

4. What should I do if I forget my portal password?

Click the Forgot Password link on the login screen to receive a time-limited reset link at your registered email address.

5. How do I contact technical support for urgent issues?

Submit a helpdesk ticket through the Support tab or reach out during live chat hours from Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
