The Art of Typography

Exploring web fonts and text styles

Typography is the foundation of web design. It dictates how text is visually interpreted by the reader. When you use proper styling, elements like highlighted text stand out effectively. It is crucial to maintain a strong balance of spacing, weights, and sizes to achieve an optimal reading experience.

Furthermore, an elegant approach to type design improves accessibility. Line height and letter spacing provide room to breathe, while proper alignment ensures the layout structure feels solid and visually balanced.
